# Family Rights: Privacy Policy (redirect)

The canonical Family Rights App policy pages are served from the `gooderman932.github.io` site. The app links to
them (`src/shared/constants/disclaimers.ts` in the app repo), and they are the URLs to enter in Play Console:

- **Privacy policy:** https://gooderman932.github.io/privacy-policy.html
- **Account deletion:** https://gooderman932.github.io/account-delete.html

This repo only redirects to those pages, so there is exactly one policy to keep accurate. Edit the policy in the
`gooderman932.github.io` repo, and keep it consistent with `PRIVACY.md` in the app repo and your Play Console
**Data safety** answers.
