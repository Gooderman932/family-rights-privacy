1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information such as your name and email address, if you create an account.
  • Content you enter such as notes, documents, dates, messages or other records you choose to store in the App. This content may include sensitive information about you or your family. You control what you enter.
  • Support communications when you contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data, used to keep the App stable.
  • Usage information such as features used and screens viewed, collected in aggregated or pseudonymous form.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS or microphone data. If a future version asks for a device permission, the App will request it at the time it is needed and you can decline.

Purchases

If the App offers paid features, payments are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of your purchase and subscription status, but not your full payment card details.

3. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that host data, provide authentication, crash reporting or analytics and process payments (for example Google Play) on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing services to us.
  • For legal reasons when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users or others.
  • In a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.
  • With your consent or at your direction, for example when you export or share a document from the App.

4. Data retention

We keep account information and content for as long as your account is active. Diagnostic data is kept for up to 90 days. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal, tax or fraud-prevention obligations. Backups are overwritten on a rolling cycle of no more than 35 days.

5. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. In the App: open Settings and choose Delete account, if available in your version; or
  2. By email: send a message to malcolmgoodmen@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Family Rights account" from the email address linked to your account.

We will confirm deletion within 30 days. Uninstalling the App alone does not delete data stored on our servers. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest on our hosting providers, access controls based on least privilege, and logging of administrative access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a portable copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do neither);
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

These rights include those under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), other U.S. state privacy laws, and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation. To exercise them, contact us at malcolmgoodmen@gmail.com. We will verify your request using information associated with your account and respond within the time required by law (generally 30 to 45 days). You may use an authorized agent, and you may appeal a decision by replying to our response. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Information that adult users choose to enter about their own children is controlled by that user and treated as their content under this policy. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA, UK or Switzerland.

10. Not legal advice

The App provides general information and organizational tools. It does not create an attorney-client relationship, and communications through the App are not protected by attorney-client privilege.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App or by email before the change takes effect.
